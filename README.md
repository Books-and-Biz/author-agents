# Books & Biz author agents

Eight complete, editable roles made for indie authors. Each role has named recurring routines, a sample output, a run log, correction rules, and setup guides for Codex, Claude, Dots, Grok Bots, and Muse. The free kits are MIT licensed and remain usable without Lab Premium. Your agent account and usage are separate.

**New here?** [Choose a role and build your install prompt](https://books-and-biz.github.io/author-agents/). The public source and ZIP below contain every free routine. [Join the free Lab classroom](https://www.skool.com/indie-author-plugin-lab/classroom/5b917301) for the same ZIP and a short setup walkthrough.

## Choose one job

| Free role | What it does | Routines |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Author Weekly Desk](roles/author-weekly-desk/INSTALL.md) | Plan the author's week from confirmed books, deadlines, capacity, and tasks; close the loop on Friday. | monday-plan, friday-review |
| [Book Page Watch](roles/book-page-watch/INSTALL.md) | Check author-supplied public retailer and reader-path URLs for breakage and visible fact changes. | page-check, monthly-summary |
| [Launch Deadline Desk](roles/launch-deadline-desk/INSTALL.md) | Keep one book launch's confirmed dates, dependencies, and missing decisions visible. | weekly-launch-map, deadline-check, launch-closeout |
| [Reader Question Desk](roles/reader-question-desk/INSTALL.md) | Turn author-supplied, anonymized reader questions into a useful FAQ and unsent answer drafts. | question-triage, faq-refresh |
| [Review Signal Desk](roles/review-signal-desk/INSTALL.md) | Summarize recurring themes in reviews the author supplies without treating reviews as orders to rewrite a book. | weekly-themes, monthly-observation |
| [Newsletter Idea Desk](roles/newsletter-idea-desk/INSTALL.md) | Keep a small list of reader newsletter ideas from confirmed author news and evergreen book material. | weekly-idea-list, monthly-angle-review |
| [Backlist Opportunity Desk](roles/backlist-opportunity-desk/INSTALL.md) | Spot modest, evidence-backed opportunities to refresh older books from the author's own catalog and supplied results. | monthly-backlist-pass, quarterly-recheck |
| [Author Site Path Watch](roles/author-site-path-watch/INSTALL.md) | Check the author's public website paths from book page to signup or purchase destination. | weekly-path-check, monthly-path-summary |

Start with [Author Weekly Desk](roles/author-weekly-desk/INSTALL.md) if you are unsure. It turns your tasks and deadlines into a Monday top-three brief and reviews progress Friday. For public book links, start with [Book Page Watch](roles/book-page-watch/INSTALL.md).

## Install in two steps

1. [Download the repository ZIP](https://github.com/Books-and-Biz/author-agents/archive/refs/heads/main.zip), extract it, and pick **one** folder under `roles/`. You can also download the same ZIP from the free Lab classroom.
2. Open that role's `INSTALL-PROMPT.md`, replace the kit and author-data paths, and paste it into your agent. Review the first manual output before adding any recurring tasks from `SCHEDULE.md`.

The kit does not create a schedule by being downloaded. Keep author data outside the kit so an update does not overwrite it. Each role's `adapters/` folder explains setup on your chosen agent app. Start with one routine and verify its first scheduled result.

## Corrections and privacy

Open a GitHub issue for a reproducible kit problem. Do not post private book files, reader details, or credentials. No routine sends, posts, spends, or changes a live account without a separate author decision.
